FAQ

Straight answers.

Something else? Email go@proximitypartners.business.

Do you guarantee I’ll be #1 on Google?

No. Nobody honestly can. Google ranks local results on relevance, distance, and reputation, and distance changes with every person searching. A business three blocks away will often outrank you for someone standing next to it.

What I can promise is the work: a complete, active, well-reviewed profile, and a monthly report that shows exactly where you stand. Anyone who guarantees #1 is guessing.

What do I need to do?

Very little. One short call at the start, a few photos if you have them, and a yes when I send you the setup to approve. After that, keep running your business. I handle the upkeep.

What access do you need?

Manager access to your Google Business Profile, which you grant from your own Google account. You stay the owner. If you don’t have a profile yet, I’ll help you create and verify one in your name.

On Proximity Books, I’ll also connect to your booking tool so review requests go out after each appointment. We’ll sort out the details on the setup call.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Both plans have a 3-month minimum, then go month to month and you can cancel any time. Your Google profile, photos, and reviews are yours and stay yours.

How fast will I see results?

Profile fixes show up on Google within days. Ranking changes take longer, usually weeks to a few months, depending on your category and how crowded your area is. That’s why there’s a 3-month minimum, and why you get a report every month so you can watch it move.
