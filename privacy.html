Privacy

Your information, plainly.

Proximity Partners is run by Breanna Gutierrez in Albuquerque, NM. This page explains what I collect, why, and who helps me handle it. Last updated October 31, 2026.

What I collect

  • From the free audit form: your name, your business name, the phone number or email you give me, and your business type.
  • From clients: the business details you send me for your setup, such as hours, services, prices, photos, and your logo, plus the Google Business Profile access you grant.
  • Public information about your business from Google Maps, such as your rating, review count, and where you rank.

How I use it

  • To run your free audit and follow up with you about it.
  • If you become a client, to deliver the service and send your monthly report.
  • I don’t sell your information, and I don’t add you to a mailing list.

Who helps me

A few service providers handle information on my behalf: Netlify (this website and its form), DataForSEO (Google Maps data), Anthropic (help drafting emails and posts, which I review before anything is sent), Resend (email delivery), Telegram (notifications to me), and PayPal (payments). They only get what they need to do that job.

How long I keep it

Audit requests and notes are kept while we’re in touch and for up to two years after, unless you ask me to delete them sooner. Client records are kept as long as I need them for the service and for tax records.

Your choices

Email go@proximitypartners.business to see what I have about you, correct it, or ask me to delete it. I’ll reply within 2 business days.
